## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-02

### Added
- Initial public version of the Chrome Manifest V3 extension.
- One-click enable/disable control.
- Local MAIN-world hook for the targeted Google Flow RPC response.
- Automatic reload of open Google Flow tabs after toggling.
- Russian-language popup interface.
